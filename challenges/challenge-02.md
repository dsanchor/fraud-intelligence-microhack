## Goal

The goal of this exercise is to build the multi-agent fraud intelligence solution.

## Actions

- Define the agents and their responsibilities.
- Connect the agents to the required data and tools.
- Run and observe an end-to-end fraud investigation.

## Success criteria

- The agents can collaborate on a fraud investigation.
- The solution produces a traceable investigation result.
- You have validated the expected end-to-end flow.
